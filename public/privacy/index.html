Privacy Policy

Utasync (歌sync) — learn languages through music

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Utasync is a free, open-source, offline-first web app. It was designed so that your library, audio files, and practice data stay on your device: there is no account, no tracking, and no server of ours to send anything to. This policy explains what the app stores locally and which third-party services your browser may contact when you choose to use particular features.

Summary

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal details are required — or even accepted.
  • No analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash-reporting services are built in.
  • We operate no backend: there is no Utasync server that could receive your data.
  • Songs, lyrics, audio, and settings are stored locally in your browser.
  • Some optional features contact third-party services (YouTube, LRCLIB, lyrics.ovh, Apple’s iTunes Search API, Hugging Face).
  • AI features run on your device, after models are downloaded; your audio is never uploaded for processing.

Information stored on your device

When you use Utasync, the app stores the following locally in your browser. None of it is transmitted to us:

  • Song library — titles, artists, lyrics, sync timing, translations, and related metadata (IndexedDB).
  • Uploaded audio — files you choose to import (Origin Private File System, where supported).
  • App preferences — playback speed, volume, display and practice options, and similar settings (local storage).
  • Cached AI models — Whisper, embedding, and vocal-separation model files, plus the ONNX Runtime they execute on, downloaded the first time you use those features (browser cache storage).
  • Cached dictionary data — Japanese and English dictionary and reading data downloaded by the service worker so readings and word lookup work offline after first use (browser cache storage).

You are in control of all of it: delete individual songs from Settings, clear the AI model cache from Settings, or remove the app’s site data through your browser to erase everything. Nothing survives on our side, because there is no “our side.”

No server, no tracking

Utasync has no backend. When you use the hosted version, the app is delivered to you as static files; like any website, the host serving those files may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and user agent) for security and operations. We do not receive, aggregate, or analyse them, and we run no analytics of any kind.

The app sets no tracking cookies. It uses browser storage — IndexedDB, the Origin Private File System, local storage, and the cache storage described above — purely to make the app work, and that data is not sent anywhere. You can self-host Utasync yourself; in that case these Terms and this policy apply to your own deployment’s storage, not to us.

Third-party services

Depending on how you use Utasync, your browser may contact:

  • YouTube — when you add or play a YouTube link, the app may request public metadata (title, artist) and available caption tracks. YouTube’s own policies apply to that service.
  • LRCLIB — when you search for synced lyrics, the app sends song title and artist text to lrclib.net to find matching lyrics.
  • lyrics.ovh — when LRCLIB does not have plain lyrics for a song, the app may send title and artist text to api.lyrics.ovh, which sources lyrics from public lyric sites.
  • Apple (iTunes Search API) — when the app looks for cover artwork, it sends the song title and artist to itunes.apple.com and downloads the matching artwork, under Apple’s terms.
  • Hugging Face — when you first use on-device AI features, the app downloads open machine-learning models from Hugging Face to your browser cache. No account with Hugging Face is required, and nothing about you is sent.
  • Substack — only if you follow the optional newsletter link from Settings. That opens substack.com in a new tab; the app itself sends nothing, and anything you provide as a reader or subscriber is handled by Substack under their policies, not by us.

The app also links out to lyric and dictionary sites (for example uta-net.com, utaten.com, animelyrics.com, lyricstranslate.com, or jisho.org) so you can compare a source yourself. Those are ordinary links: nothing is sent to those sites unless you open one.

On-device processing

Features such as auto-align, transcription, word-pair colouring, and vocal isolation run in your browser using the downloaded models described above. Audio you analyse is processed locally and is not uploaded anywhere.

Children’s privacy

Utasync is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information — there is no account, no sign-in, and no server — there is nothing for us to receive from a child or anyone else. If you have a concern about how the app handles something on your device, contact us and we will help where we can.

International users

Utasync is available globally, and your local data stays on your device wherever you are. The third-party services listed above may process requests in other countries under their own terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. These pages are part of the public source, so their history — what changed, and when — is visible in the repository as well.
