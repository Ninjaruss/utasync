The service

Utasync is a browser-based application for language learning through music. It lets you import or link songs, sync and edit lyrics, practice with bilingual display tools, and use optional on-device AI features. Everything runs in your browser: the app has no user accounts and no backend of its own, and it works offline after the assets a given song needs have been downloaded once.
